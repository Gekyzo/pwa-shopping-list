    Summary

    The screens agree on the look but not on the rules: 17 conflicts, 5 of them in the tokens themselves. The plan follows the phone screens and the Tailwind palette, and keeps the radius scale and the type of design.md.

    1. The tokens of the screens are not the tokens of design.md. Palette, radius scale and the primary colour differ; 7 of 10 screens have no dark variant (tokens).

    3. The frame changes on every screen. Four brand names, swapped nav icons, five forms of "offline" (frame); the plan takes one value for each (decisions).

    P1

    #1Palette of the screens against design.md

    design.md:13-17

    The front matter of design.md is the stone edit (on-surface-variant #78716c, outline #d6d3d1, outline-variant #e7e5e4). utilitarian_market_ledger/DESIGN.md and all ten Tailwind configs hold #3f4942, #6f7a71, #bec9c0. Both files keep surface #fff8f5 while their prose names #F9F8F5.

    Proposal: Task 0 sets the tokens to the Tailwind values and DESIGN.md to the values of utilitarian_market_ledger/DESIGN.md.

    P1

    #2Radius scale shifted by one step

    Each Tailwind key holds the value of the key below it in design.md. A chip with rounded-full is 12px, a card with rounded-xl is 8px, a field with rounded is 2px.

    Proposal: keep design.md: 6px controls, pills, a round 32px tick.

    P2

    #005937 and #13734b both fill primary buttons. design.md names #13734B the primary action colour.

    Proposal: --done/--primary = #13734b; #005937 only for pressed and text on tint.
