Privacy

Bodega, the shopping list app. Last updated 24 October 2026.

Bodega is a personal app. One person runs it for family and friends. It has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Without an account

Your lists, recipes, menu and settings stay in the storage of your browser on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

With an account

An account is optional. It keeps your data in sync between your devices. The server stores:

  • your email address and, if you give one, your name;
  • a hash of your password, if you sign in with a password;
  • if you sign in with Google: your Google account ID, email address and name. Bodega asks Google only for these (the openid, email and profile scopes). It has no access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data;
  • whether the administrator approved your account;
  • your lists, recipes, menu and settings, with the time of each change;
  • a log of requests to the server: time, address, IP address and browser. The log is deleted after 5 days.

The app keeps your sign-in in the storage of your browser. It sets no cookies.

Where the data is

The server is a Hetzner machine in Nuremberg, Germany (European Union). A backup is made every night and kept for 14 days.

Who can see it

Only you, through the app, and the administrator who runs Bodega. The data is not sold, shared or used for any other purpose.

Email

Bodega sends two kinds of email, through Gmail:

  • to the administrator, when someone asks for an account;
  • to you, when your account is approved.

Delete your data

Reply to any email from Bodega and ask for deletion. Your account and all its data are then deleted from the server. Copies in backups expire within 14 days. Data on your device stays until you clear it with Delete all data in the settings of the app, or in your browser.
