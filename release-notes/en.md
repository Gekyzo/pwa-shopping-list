# Release notes

What changes for you in each version of the app. The newest version is first.

## Unreleased

- When you accept a shared list, your own list stays. `Shopping` shows both side by side: `All`, `My list` and the other person's list are chips at the top, each with what is left to buy. In `All`, each item shows its list, and what you add goes to your list. When recipes and the menu are shared too, `Recipes` and `Menu` show those of both lists, each with its list: a shared recipe sends its products to the shared list.
- When you invite someone in `Compartir lista`, you choose what you share: `Shopping only` or `Shopping, Recipes and Menu`. You can change it later with a tap on the person. When someone declines the invite or stops sharing, you see `Cancelled` and can `Invite again`.

## 1.5.0 - 2026-10-25

- You can share your list with other people: in `Ajustes` › `Cuenta` › `Compartir lista`, type their email. Once they accept, they see and change the same list, recipes and menu on their devices.

## 1.4.1 - 2026-10-24

- When the server asks you to wait a few seconds while a long list uploads, the app no longer shows that you are offline. It tries again by itself.
- In `Settings` › `Account`, the email and the time of the last sync no longer overlap on a phone: the time is under the email, and a long email wraps to more lines.
- The “Offline” note now goes by itself when a sync reaches the server, also when the phone does not report that the network is back, and when you return to the app.

## 1.4.0 - 2026-10-24

- Log in to keep your lists on all your devices. Request access with your email or with Google; the administrator approves each account. A change on one device reaches the others in seconds, and offline your changes are saved and sent later. If you already have a list, it stays on the device, and the notice `Log in to sync` takes you to the login.
- `Settings` › `Help` › `What's new` lists what changes in each version. After an update, it opens by itself once.
