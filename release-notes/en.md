# Release notes

What changes for you in each version of the app. The newest version is first.

## Unreleased

- Log in to keep your lists on all your devices. Request access with your email or with Google; Ciro approves each account. A change on one device reaches the others in seconds, and offline your changes are saved and sent later. If you already have a list, it stays on the device, and the notice `Log in to sync` takes you to the login.
- `Settings` › `Help` › `What's new` lists what changes in each version. After an update, it opens by itself once.
