# Release notes

What changes for you in each version of the app. The newest version is first.

## Unreleased

No change.

## 1.13.1 - 2026-11-09

- On a phone, **Shopping** looks more like the design: a bar at the top with the **Finish shopping** button (in place of the three dots), a dock with the shop at the left and no arrows, taller rows with a larger quantity, and **Bought** open from the start. A bought item no longer shows the grip: **Edit** is still there when you slide it. The message with **Undo** shows at the top, with a check mark.
- On wide screens, the **Recipes** header looks more like the design: the search has its own line, the **Filters** button shows or hides the filters, and below the title you see whether the recipes are kept on this device or synced.
- On wide screens, **Recipes** looks more like the design: each recipe is a card with its buttons at the foot, and the panel of a recipe has the **Add to Shopping** button, which says how many products it adds. On every screen, a recipe shows how many of its products are in the list and marks each one with a green dot.
- In **Menu**, on screens wider than a phone, the **Previous weeks** panel shows the last three weeks with meals, each with a **Repeat this week** button. The **Automatic basket** card says how many products **Fill basket** adds, and replaces the **For the basket** panel.
- On wide screens, **Menu** looks more like the design: each day is a card, each meal shows its kind in a small tile and its status in a pill, and the week summary has a progress bar.
- In **Menu**, the › arrow goes to the next weeks, up to four weeks ahead. You can plan their meals, fill the basket from them or empty them, as with this week.
- On wide screens, **Settings** has the desktop design: one centred column, each group below its title.
- On wide screens, the add buttons no longer disappear when a sheet opens, so the header and the sidebar do not move.
- On wide screens, the shop filters of **Shopping** are lower.
- On wide screens, **Recipes**, **Menu** and **Settings** use the full width, as **Shopping** does.
- On wide screens, a small card below the panels of **Shopping** shows how much space your lists take on the device.
- On wide screens, the panels of **Shopping** follow the design more closely: **See** has an arrow, the names in **Next trip** are less bold and the button that moves everything shows a list icon.
- On wide screens, the header of **Shopping** follows the design: on the page background, a larger title, buttons with squarer corners and shops in smaller text. The add button says **Add**.
- With a mouse, the ⋮ button of an item no longer covers its quantity: it has its own place before the handle.
- With a mouse, a click on an item of **Shopping** opens **Edit**. To tick it, click its circle. A drag with the mouse still moves the item. With a finger, a tap on the item still ticks it.
- On wide screens of about 1024 px, the title of the header is no longer cut: the search field gets narrower and, when needed, shows only the magnifier.
- On wide screens, the list and the panels of **Shopping** follow the design: more room around the list and between the groups, wider panels, lighter tags and quantities, and a thicker green bar for each recipe.
- Each section has its own address: `#/compra`, `#/recetas`, `#/menu` and `#/ajustes`. A reload keeps you in the same section. The back button of the phone goes to **Shopping** first, then leaves the app.
- On wide screens, the sidebar follows the design: a little wider, the current section on a stronger background with a green bar, the count of items in a pill and the state of the sync below a line.
- With a keyboard: 1 to 4 open the sections, N adds and / searches. On a selected item, the menu key or Shift+F10 shows its actions and Delete deletes it.
- With a mouse, right-click an item or use its ⋮ button to see its actions. Dragging the header with the mouse no longer changes the section.
- In **Recipes**, each product you add starts with a capital letter: “rice” is saved as “Rice”.
- On wide screens, **Shopping** fills the whole width. The sidebar has a soft background, a status line below **Bodega**, the add button and, at the foot, a card with the state of the sync.
- On wide screens, the rows of **Shopping** follow the design: a more compact name, “every trip” beside the name and the quantity in a fixed-width font. Each group says how many items it has left (“3 items”). The pencil of each row is now a six-dot handle; a tap on it opens **Edit**.
- The header of **Shopping** says how much is left to buy in green, with no bar. On wide screens it has a search field (opens with Ctrl+K or ⌘K), the **Finish shopping** button and the shops below the title.
- In **Shopping**, you can postpone an item to the next trip: slide the row or use **Postpone** in **Edit**. On wide screens, **Next trip** lists them with a + to bring them back, and **Recipes in the list** shows how many ingredients of each recipe you already have.
- New colours: a warm paper background with soft green-grey lines, in the light and the dark theme.
- In **Shopping**, each item name has more room and keeps one line, and its quantity is a soft chip.
- In **Shopping**, the bought products stand in a card of their own, under a check.
- The buttons at the bottom of **Shopping** float over the list, and the message after a tick is dark.
- **Edit** shows each field as a box with its name inside, the repeat switch as a card, and the shops as pills you add with + and remove with ×.
- In **Recipes**, the buttons of a recipe stand beside its name, and its products take the whole width of the card.
- **Menu** shows how many meals of the week are planned and how many are free, an icon on each meal for a recipe or a dish, and a calendar on the week.
- **Settings** shows an icon on each group, marks **Delete all data** as permanent, and explains **Install the app**.
- On a wide screen, the current section stands out with a green bar, and each group of **Shopping** has its title inside its card.

## 1.13.0 - 2026-11-05

- The app moves to `bodega.artisandevs.duckdns.org`. On the old address, **Move my data** copies your list to the new one; with an account, it sends your changes first, and then you sign in there.

## 1.12.1 - 2026-11-03

- A new look: a warm paper background, the larger Hanken Grotesk font, round checkboxes and an emerald green for bought items and the main action.
- `Recipes`, `Menu` and `Settings` follow the new look: a green button sends a recipe to the list, meals are taller cards and the arrows of `Settings` are easier to see.
- The bar shows cutlery for `Recipes` and an open book for `Menu`, and actions that delete data are red.
- Each row of `Settings` starts with an icon, and the head of each store section shows an icon of its kind: fruit, dairy, bread, cleaning and more.
- In `Menu`, the week bar shows its days and each day shows its date. Each meal says if it is a recipe or a quick note, and a free meal shows a ⊕.
- In `Recipes`, each recipe shows its products and an «in the list» badge, and chips filter the recipes: `All`, `In the list` and `With a link`.

## 1.12.0 - 2026-11-03

- In a past week of **Menu**, **Repeat this week** copies its meals to the current week. If you change your mind, tap **Undo**.

## 1.11.0 - 2026-11-03

- **Menú** starts empty each Monday. Use the arrows at the top to see the menu of past weeks, up to 26 weeks back.

## 1.10.2 - 2026-10-31

- In What's new, the names of the buttons show in bold, with no asterisks.

## 1.10.1 - 2026-10-30

- On a tablet or a computer, the margins of the screen are the same as in the other apps: the list, the panels and the sheets stand at the same distance from the side menu.

## 1.10.0 - 2026-10-30

- A recipe with a link shows a ▶ button beside “Add to Shopping” that opens the video at once. In the sheet of the recipe, a card above the Link field shows “Watch video” and the site of the link, such as youtube.com.

## 1.9.1 - 2026-10-27

- The app opens again after the update to 1.9.0 when you start it from a shortcut. Updates now also arrive through the shortcuts.

## 1.9.0 - 2026-10-27

- If you share a list, an item that is in your list and in the shared list now shows as one row in All, with both tags. Each list has its own colour. A new setting chooses whether a tick or a delete of that row changes your list only or both lists.

## 1.8.0 - 2026-10-26

- On wide screens, Settings no longer shows two scroll bars.
- On a tablet, a foldable or a computer, the sections are always on the left. On a computer, Bodega sits in a centred frame with a sidebar that shows the names of the sections and what you still have to buy.
- On wide screens, Shopping shows what is left at each shop and what comes back on the next trip, and Menu shows what “Fill basket” adds.
- The title of the tab and of the installed app is now "Shopping list - Bodega".
- On a tablet or a computer, a recipe opens in a panel next to the list of recipes, and you can move from one to another without closing it.
- On a tablet, a foldable or a computer, the add button is in the header, and sheets such as Add open next to the list without covering it.
- On wide screens, Settings shows each group with its explanation beside it.

## 1.7.1 - 2026-10-26

- When you search in Shopping, the list stays at the top and no longer moves down to the search field.
- If someone invites you to share their list while you share yours, you can now remove those people in the invite itself and then accept it.

## 1.7.0 - 2026-10-25

- You can use the app without an account: tap **Use without an account** on Sign in. Your lists stay on this device. Sign in later from Settings to sync them.
- Sign-in with email and password is gone. If you used it, sign in with Google with the same email: you keep your account and your lists.

## 1.6.0 - 2026-10-25

- When you accept a shared list, your own list stays. `Shopping` shows both side by side: `All`, `My list` and the other person's list are chips at the top, each with what is left to buy. In `All`, each item shows its list, and what you add goes to your list. When recipes and the menu are shared too, `Recipes` and `Menu` show those of both lists, each with its list: a shared recipe sends its products to the shared list.
- When you invite someone in `Compartir lista`, you choose what you share: `Shopping only` or `Shopping, Recipes and Menu`. You can change it later with a tap on the person. When someone declines the invite or stops sharing, you see `Cancelled` and can `Invite again`.

## 1.5.0 - 2026-10-25

- You can share your list with other people: in `Ajustes` › `Cuenta` › `Compartir lista`, type their email. Once they accept, they see and change the same list, recipes and menu on their devices.

## 1.4.1 - 2026-10-24

- When the server asks you to wait a few seconds while a long list uploads, the app no longer shows that you are offline. It tries again by itself.
- In `Settings` › `Account`, the email and the time of the last sync no longer overlap on a phone: the time is under the email, and a long email wraps to more lines.
- The “Offline” note now goes by itself when a sync reaches the server, also when the phone does not report that the network is back, and when you return to the app.

## 1.4.0 - 2026-10-24

- Log in to keep your lists on all your devices. Request access with your email or with Google; the administrator approves each account. A change on one device reaches the others in seconds, and offline your changes are saved and sent later. If you already have a list, it stays on the device, and the notice `Log in to sync` takes you to the login.
- `Settings` › `Help` › `What's new` lists what changes in each version. After an update, it opens by itself once.
