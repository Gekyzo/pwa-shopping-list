# Roadmap

Planned features, not in order of priority. `CHANGELOG.md` records what shipped.

## Planned

- **Menu planner**. Based on recipes. Auto fill products basket.
- **Change theme**. Swap theme.
- **Skip intro**. Beside the next button.
- **Enable modules in settings**. Use feature flags to enable/disable modules.
- **Browser database.** Store the data in the browser database instead of
  `localStorage`.
- **Price estimation.** Estimate the price of a list.
- **Shared lists.** Share lists with contacts.
- **Store sections.** Add the category or section of the store to each product.
  The sections are configurable in the settings.
- **Quantities.** Add a quantity to each item.
- **Multiple lists.** Keep more lists than `Actual` and `Next`, each with its name.
- **Split by shop.** Split a list into one list for each shop of its items.
